# Botanical brand exploration

2026-10-02. Built-in imagegen, transparent PNG. Botanical photograph provided as a morphology reference; the generated emblem is an artistic interpretation.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand / botanical identity.
Create a NEW elegant, original logo emblem for "勿忘我 / Remember Me", a gentle personal audio-memory journal whose UI uses creamy paper, sage green and natural landscapes. The attached real Myosotis sylvatica photograph is BOTANICAL REFERENCE ONLY: study the five broad, shallow, rounded lobes, the tiny warm yellow throat and pale collar. Do not reproduce the photograph or its background/composition.
Design a compact botanical emblem that feels like a carefully drawn artist's signature, NOT a generic radial blue flower icon. One clearly recognizable five-lobed forget-me-not bloom sits on a single flowing sage-green stem that curves below it in a graceful, asymmetrical open loop, suggesting a remembered trace. A small leaf and one tiny unopened bud make the botanical gesture complete; keep the overall silhouette compact, calm and distinctive. Main bloom must remain the visual focus, with exactly FIVE broad softly irregular petals, a very small warm ochre/gold eye, and a delicate ivory throat ring. Petals use low-saturation dusty blue, with restrained tonal variation; stem and leaf use muted deep sage. No saturated cobalt blue, no hard black outlines, no long teardrop petals, no six-petal flower, no daisy, no extra flowers. Use refined flat cut-paper / screenprinted botanical illustration: a few clear shapes, graceful controlled contours, tiny subtle human asymmetry, almost no texture, no photorealistic rendering, no 3D or drop shadow. Stroke thickness must remain readable when reduced to a small mobile brand mark; avoid hairlines, filigree, microdetails or decorative speckles.
One isolated emblem, centered, fills about 80 percent of the square canvas with generous even margins. Truly transparent background including all open spaces. No text, no lettering, no mockup, no border, no layout sheet. The result should feel quietly poetic, relaxed, natural, and professional.
